CHARACTER GUIDE

The people who make Kasukabe unforgettable.

Shin-chan is the center of the chaos, while Hiroshi and Misae try to keep the Nohara household moving. Himawari and Shiro add their own brand of trouble and loyalty.

His school friends Kazama, Nene, Masao and Bo-chan form the core of the Kasukabe Defence Force. When superhero mode begins, Action Kamen, Buri Buri Zaemon and Kantam Robo enter the picture.

TIMELINE

From home to hero mode.

🏠 Nohara mode

Family life, shopping trips, school mornings and the classic “Shin-chan, stop that!” moments.

🎒 School mode

Futaba Kindergarten brings Kazama, Nene, Masao and Bo-chan into the daily chaos.

🦸 Hero mode

Action Kamen becomes Shin-chan's favorite hero, while Buri Buri Zaemon brings his own unpredictable style.

🍫 Chocobi mode

Every adventure becomes more serious when Shin-chan discovers a chance to get Chocobi.
